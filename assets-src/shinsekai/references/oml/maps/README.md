# 1912 Osaka survey map originals

These are original-size JPEGs from Osaka Municipal Library's *實測大阪地図* (Shoryudo, 1912). Codex checked **each individual record** for its management number and the notices `申請不要・二次利用可` and `CC0（CC0 1.0...` before download. `scripts/fetch-shinsekai-map1912.cjs` repeats those checks. The library's CC0 statement permits reuse; retain the item link and library credit with derivative maps.

Both survey sheets are 7288 × 9941 pixels. The Shinsekai sheet is 28,323,516 bytes; the Tennoji sheet is 29,906,061 bytes. The index is 9963 × 7092 pixels and 46,868,329 bytes. The source maps depict a land survey, not an attraction or building plan. The index legend has **no separate dashed-circle symbol** and no obvious elevation-benchmark symbol. A dashed circle south of the semicircular frontage may mark a planned feature or a round pond; it does not identify a tower footprint by itself. These scans are research inputs outside the website build. Do not render their parcel lines or any derived first-tower coordinate as surveyed geometry before georeferencing them to surviving road/rail controls.
