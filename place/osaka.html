Osaka Castle

大阪城

Compare on the map

What to look for

Start with Osaka Castle and its moats. Compare the land outside the walls with the park and railway corridors visible on the current map.

A castle has stood here since the 1580s. The tower you see now was rebuilt in 1931.

During the war, a huge weapons factory filled the ground around the castle. It was bombed heavily in 1945.

In the old photo you can see the scarred, empty ground. The castle tower itself came through it.

Slide to today and that same ground is a wide green park.
