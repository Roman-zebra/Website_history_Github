Seafood by the Kanmon Strait; check food-event dates.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

A wholesale and retail fish market by the Kanmon Strait, known for fugu. On Fridays, weekends and public holidays, sushi and seafood stalls fill the market floor for the Iki-iki Bakangai event.
