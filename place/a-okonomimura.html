Okonomimura

広島・お好み村

Compare on the map

Watch Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki take shape on the griddle.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

About 25 okonomiyaki counters on the second to fourth floors of one building, carrying on a history that began with post-war food stalls. Hours and days off vary by shop.
