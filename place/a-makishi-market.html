🍜 Markets and food streets

Makishi Public Market

那覇・第一牧志公設市場

Compare on the map

Okinawan ingredients and market meals in central Naha.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Buy fish or meat on the ground floor and have it cooked at the restaurants upstairs; the market calls this mochiage. Closed on the fourth Sunday of the month (except December) and at New Year, Lunar New Year and Obon.
