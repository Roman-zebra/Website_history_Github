🛍️ Shopping streets

Kurashiki Bikan District

倉敷美観地区

Compare on the map

Denim, textiles and crafts among canal-side storehouses.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

White-walled storehouses and Western-style buildings along the Kurashiki River. Kojima, also part of Kurashiki, is known as the birthplace of Japanese-made jeans.
