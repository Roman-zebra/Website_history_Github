A 47m concrete mine tower standing alone in a housing estate.

A 47m concrete winding tower with nothing around it. The mine went in 1964 and the housing estate grew up to its feet, so it stands in a suburb with no explanation.

About Shime, Fukuoka

Shime (志免町, Shime-machi) is a town located in Kasuya District, Fukuoka Prefecture, Japan. As of 1 April 2024, the town had an estimated population of 46,388 in 21035 households, and a population density of 5300 persons per km². The total area of the town is 8.69 km2 (3.36 sq mi)

日本語

住宅地の中に一基だけ残る高さ47mの竪坑櫓。

高さ47mのコンクリート竪坑櫓が、何も無い中に立っています。1964年に炭鉱が消え、住宅地が足元まで広がったため、説明のないまま郊外に残っている。

志免町（しめまち）は、福岡県福岡市近郊の町。糟屋郡に属し、福岡都市圏の一部である。
