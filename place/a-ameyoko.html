🛍️ Shopping streets

Ameyoko

上野・アメ横

Compare on the map

A lively market street for snacks, clothing and bargains.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

It began as a black market just after the Second World War. Around 400 shops fill some 500 metres between JR Ueno and Okachimachi stations, and the crowds in late December are a yearly sight.
