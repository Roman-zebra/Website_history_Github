Jozankei View Hotel — Lagoon

定山渓ビューホテル・水の王国ラグーン

Compare on the map

A pastel indoor water park in Jozankei, where childhood holiday colours meet a repeating ceiling grid.

Lagoon is the indoor heated water park at Jozankei View Hotel in Sapporo, Hokkaido. A public post by ぼっち on 30 September 2026 introduced its dreamcore atmosphere.

Reading the posted photographs: a pink mushroom-shaped fountain, a yellow stem and colourful fish sit beneath rows of white ceiling beams. Cool blue water and warm floor colours reflect each other. The cheerful decoration and unusually quiet framing suggest a remembered holiday seen in a dream; this is an interpretation of the photographs, not a claim that the pool is always empty.
