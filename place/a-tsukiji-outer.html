Tsukiji Outer Market

築地場外市場

Compare on the map

Sushi, seafood bowls and specialist food shops in Tokyo.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

When the wholesale market and its fish auctions moved out of Tsukiji, the shops around it stayed. The outer market is a mix of wholesalers, retailers and restaurants.
