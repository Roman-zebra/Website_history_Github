Osu Shopping District

名古屋・大須商店街

Compare on the map

Vintage fashion, electronics and pop-culture finds near Osu Kannon.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

The streets around Osu Kannon temple, south of Sakae: vintage clothing, electronics and otaku shops. A flea market is held at the temple on the 18th and 28th of every month.
