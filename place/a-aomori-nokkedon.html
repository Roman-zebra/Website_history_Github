Aomori Furukawa Fish Market

青森魚菜センター・古川市場

Compare on the map

Choose seafood toppings for your own nokkedon rice bowl.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Buy a sheet of tickets at the information desk, get rice at a shop with an orange flag, then spend the rest of the tickets on toppings from the stalls. Five minutes’ walk from Aomori Station’s east exit.
