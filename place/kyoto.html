Kyoto — Kiyomizu-dera

京都・清水寺

Compare on the map

What to look for

Use Kiyomizu-dera as an anchor between the wooded hillside and the streets of Higashiyama. Look for the boundary between temple grounds, trees and the built-up city.

Kyoto was the capital of Japan for over a thousand years.

Kiyomizu-dera stands on a wooden stage built out over the hillside. It was rebuilt in 1633 without using nails.

Kyoto escaped the heavy bombing that hit other cities, so many old wooden buildings are still standing.

Compare the available historical imagery with today. Look for both continuity and change in the temple grounds and the surrounding streets.
