Kanazawa

金沢

Compare on the map

What to look for

Compare the castle area and Kenroku-en with the surrounding streets. Look for contrasts between broad grounds, narrow lanes and waterways.

Kanazawa was one of the richest towns in Japan under the samurai lords, and it was never bombed in the war.

So whole districts of Edo-period streets survive: samurai houses, teahouse quarters, narrow lanes and water channels.

Kenroku-en, next to the castle, is counted among the three great gardens of Japan.

Almost all of Japan's gold leaf is still beaten here. You can eat ice cream covered in it.
