Dotonbori Food Streets

大阪・道頓堀

Compare on the map

Takoyaki and okonomiyaki beside Osaka’s neon canal.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Streets beside a canal about 400 years old, with the Glico sign towering over Ebisubashi Bridge. Takoyaki, okonomiyaki and crab restaurants; busiest after dark.
