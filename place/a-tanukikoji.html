Tanukikoji Shopping Street

札幌・狸小路商店街

Compare on the map

Souvenirs and everyday shops in a covered Sapporo arcade.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

One of Hokkaido’s oldest shopping streets, trading since the early Meiji era. Seven blocks and about 900 metres, with some 200 shops, all under an arcade roof.
