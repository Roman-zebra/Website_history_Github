Qua Palace / クアパレス

35.7134, 140.0472

Open Qua Palace on the map →

A neighbourhood bathhouse dressed up as a European palace.

A sento is somewhere you pass through on the way home: pay, wash, soak, leave. This one was an ordinary post-war bathhouse until 1989, when it was remodelled as a white "palace", with marble-look statues outside and chandeliers inside. Everything a local bathhouse needs is still there and still works; it has simply been dressed as somewhere it is not, on a quiet residential road near a suburban station.

⚠ Liminal describes a feeling, not permission. This is a working bathhouse: go in as a paying bather during its opening hours, and do not photograph inside the baths.

About Qua Palace

Qua Palace is a public bathhouse (sento) in Yakuendai 4-chōme, Funabashi, Chiba, about seven minutes on foot from Narashino Station. Local press reports that it opened shortly after the war as Fujimi-yu and was remodelled into its present form in 1989; the name joins the German Kur (a cure) with "palace". The white building is decorated with Italian-made, marble-look statues of lions and women, and visitor reports describe a Versailles-style lobby with chandeliers, and baths that include a jet bath, a sauna and a cold bath.

日本語

ヨーロッパの宮殿のように飾られた、住宅街の銭湯。

銭湯は、帰り道に立ち寄って通り過ぎる場所です。ここは戦後まもなくからの普通の銭湯でしたが、1989年（平成元年）に、外には大理石調の像、中にはシャンデリアのある白い「宮殿」へ改装されました。街の銭湯としての設備はそのまま動いていて、ただ、郊外の駅に近い静かな住宅街の中で、どこか別の場所の姿をしている。

クアパレスは、千葉県船橋市薬円台4丁目にある銭湯です。習志野駅から徒歩約7分。地元紙の取材によると、戦後まもなく「富士見湯」として営業を始め、平成元年（1989年）に現在の姿へ改装されました。名前の「クア」はドイツ語で療養、「パレス」は宮殿を意味します。白い建物の外にはイタリア製の大理石調のライオンや女性の像が並び、訪問記では、シャンデリアのあるベルサイユ宮殿風のロビーや、ジェットバス・サウナ・水風呂などが紹介されています。
