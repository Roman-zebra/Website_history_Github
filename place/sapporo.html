札幌

Compare on the map

What to look for

Find the grid of streets around central Sapporo. Follow a straight road across several blocks and compare open spaces, parks and the spread of buildings.

Sapporo is young. Most Japanese cities grew slowly over centuries, but this one was laid out on a grid in 1869, when Hokkaido was being settled.

In the old photo the grid is already there — but look between the blocks. There is still open ground and farmland.

Slide to today and the same streets are packed solid with buildings.

Hokkaido is the snowy north. Every February the city fills a whole street with giant snow sculptures.
