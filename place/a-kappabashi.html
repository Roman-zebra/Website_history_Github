🛍️ Shopping streets

Kappabashi Kitchen Town

かっぱ橋道具街

Compare on the map

Knives, tableware and wonderfully realistic food samples.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Tool and second-hand dealers began setting up here in 1912. Today it is Tokyo’s street for kitchen and restaurant equipment.
