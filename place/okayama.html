Okayama Castle

岡山城

Compare on the map

What to look for

Follow the Asahi River around Okayama Castle. Compare the castle grounds and riverbanks before looking at the streets beyond them.

This castle is nicknamed the Crow Castle because its walls are painted black.

In 1934 a typhoon pushed the river over its banks and broke the castle's inner moat. Water covered the whole city.

Someone set a stone here to record it, so that people would not forget how high the water reached.

Then in 1945 the city burned in an air raid. You can see the burnt ground in the old photo.
