Kuromon Market

大阪・黒門市場

Compare on the map

Explore Osaka’s food stalls and fresh seafood.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

About 150 shops along some 580 metres, trading since around 1822–23. Fresh fish, fruit and vegetables, with restaurants among the stalls. Hours vary by shop.
