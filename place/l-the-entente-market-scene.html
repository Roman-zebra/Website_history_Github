The Entente Market Scene

ジ・アンタンテ マーケットシーン

Compare on the map

A skylit indoor town on Kobe’s Rokko Island, with peach walls, glass lifts and layered walkways.

Market Scene is an operating shopping complex at 5-15 Koyocho-naka, Higashinada, Kobe. A post by Saho on 5 October 2026 shows its atrium; the author names this location in a follow-up reply.

Reading the posted photographs: a glazed roof grid lights a tall void framed by peach tiles and teal accents. A glass lift shaft, bridges and stairs layer several routes above stone and water. The oversized indoor street and retro colours make an everyday shopping place feel like a town inside a dream. The quiet frame does not establish that the facility is deserted.

⚠ Liminal describes a feeling, not permission. Look from public roads and open visitor areas; do not enter closed buildings, ruins, fenced or private land. A pin on the map is not permission to go inside.

Before you go

The local area guide places Market Scene about three minutes northwest of Island Center Station. Shops have different hours. Use open public areas, keep circulation routes clear and follow photography instructions. Address: 5-15 Koyocho-naka, Higashinada, Kobe. Information checked on 5 October 2026.
