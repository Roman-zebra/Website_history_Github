Compare on the map

Browse gifts and lifestyle shops in former port warehouses.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Kanemori dates back to 1869. Its red-brick warehouses by the harbour now hold shops, a beer hall, a gallery and an event hall; the shops open from 9:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.
