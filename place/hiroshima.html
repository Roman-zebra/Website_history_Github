Hiroshima

Compare on the map

What to look for

Trace the Motoyasu River beside the Atomic Bomb Dome, then compare the surrounding blocks with Peace Memorial Park. The dome is one of the surviving atomic-bombed structures, not the only building that remained.

On 6 August 1945, the atomic bomb exploded near this site. The damaged dome was later preserved as a memorial.

The dome is one of several surviving atomic-bombed structures. Compare the surrounding city blocks, river and park to explore the changing landscape.
