🍜 Markets and food streets

Nijo Market

札幌・二条市場

Compare on the map

Seafood bowls at a market in central Sapporo.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Said to have begun in the early Meiji era, when fishermen from Ishikari brought their catch to Sapporo. About 25 seafood shops plus places to eat, seven minutes’ walk from Odori Station.
