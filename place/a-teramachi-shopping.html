Teramachi Shopping Arcade

京都・寺町京極商店街

Compare on the map

Kyoto souvenirs and specialist shops on a covered historic street.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

In 1590 Toyotomi Hideyoshi brought some 80 temples to this street, and from the late 17th century shops selling things for the temples opened in front of them. Today it is a covered street of fashion, souvenirs and specialist shops.
