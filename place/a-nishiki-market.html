🍜 Markets and food streets

Nishiki Market

京都・錦市場

Compare on the map

Pickles, dashi ingredients and Kyoto tastes under one arcade.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

A 390-metre market street with about 400 years of history, known as Kyoto’s kitchen. The market asks visitors not to eat while walking: eat in front of or inside the shop where you bought the food.
