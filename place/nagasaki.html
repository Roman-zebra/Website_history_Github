Nagasaki

長崎

Compare on the map

What to look for

Look for Nagasaki Peace Park in the valley. The selected historical layer is from the 1960s, so it shows the city after reconstruction had begun, not the immediate aftermath of 1945.

For over 200 years, when Japan was closed to the outside world, Nagasaki was almost the only port where foreign ships could come.

On 9 August 1945 an atomic bomb exploded above this valley.

The old photo here is from the 1960s, about twenty years after. The city had already been rebuilt.

Slide to today and look for the park. A single black pillar marks the point directly under the explosion.
