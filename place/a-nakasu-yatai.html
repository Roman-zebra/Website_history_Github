🍜 Markets and food streets

Nakasu Yatai

福岡・中洲の屋台

Compare on the map

Ramen and small dishes at Fukuoka’s evening food stalls.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Fukuoka has more than 100 yatai, including a row along the river at Nakasu. Ramen, oden and yakitori are the usual order.
