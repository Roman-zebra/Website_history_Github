Nakano Broadway

中野ブロードウェイ

Compare on the map

Manga, figures and vintage collectables inside a shopping complex.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Opened in 1966 as a building with shops below and homes above. About 200 shops fill the basement to the fourth floor, including shops for manga, figures and collectors’ watches.
