Hokuriku seafood and regional produce in Kanazawa.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Kanazawa’s kitchen for about 300 years: some 170 shops along narrow lanes, with seasonal fish, vegetables and fruit. Hours vary by shop.
