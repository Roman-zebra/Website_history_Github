Yokohama — Minato Mirai

横浜・みなとみらい

Compare on the map

What to look for

Follow the waterfront around Minato Mirai. Compare the outlines of docks, reclaimed land and present-day parks; the coastline is as useful a clue as the buildings.

In the old photo this is a working port, full of docks where ships were built and repaired.

The shipyard moved away, and from the 1980s the whole area was rebuilt.

Slide to today: towers, a ferris wheel, and a park sit where the docks used to be.

One old stone dock was kept and turned into a garden, so you can still find a piece of the old photo.
