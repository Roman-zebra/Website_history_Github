Hirome Market

高知・ひろめ市場

Compare on the map

Bonito tataki and Tosa dishes around shared tables.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Opened in 1998 on the site of the Hirome residence, home of a senior Tosa retainer, which gave the market its name. Open from 10 a.m. (9 a.m. on Sundays) until late evening; shop hours vary.
