Fukuoka — Hakata

福岡・博多

Compare on the map

What to look for

Explore Hakata’s streets and waterways, then widen the view towards the waterfront. Compare the size of city blocks and the shape of the built-up area.

Hakata is closer to Korea than it is to Tokyo, and it has been Japan's trading door to the Asian mainland for over a thousand years.

In June 1945 air raids burned much of the city. The old photo shows the cleared, blackened ground.

It rebuilt quickly and is now the biggest city on the island of Kyūshū.

Hakata is also associated with tonkotsu ramen and its food culture.
