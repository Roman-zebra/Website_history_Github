Hakodate Morning Market

函館朝市

Compare on the map

Start the day with seafood beside Hakodate Station.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

About 250 shops selling seafood, fruit and sweets, a minute’s walk from Hakodate Station. Hours vary by shop, but the market runs roughly from 5 a.m. to 2 p.m.
