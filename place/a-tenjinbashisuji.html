Tenjinbashisuji Shopping Street

大阪・天神橋筋商店街

Compare on the map

Everyday Osaka shopping along a long neighbourhood arcade.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Billed as Japan’s longest shopping street, it runs right by Osaka Tenmangu, the shrine of the Tenjin Festival.
