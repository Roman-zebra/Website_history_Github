Tenjin Underground Mall

福岡・天神地下街

Compare on the map

Fashion and lifestyle shops connected beneath central Fukuoka.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

An underground shopping street beneath central Tenjin. Shops are generally open from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. and restaurants until 9 p.m., with some exceptions.
