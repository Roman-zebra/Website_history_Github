🛍️ Shopping streets

Shinsaibashi-suji

大阪・心斎橋筋商店街

Compare on the map

Fashion, department stores and souvenirs in central Osaka.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

A covered arcade running from Shinsaibashi Station down to Dotonbori, described by the Japan National Tourism Organization as one of the busiest and oldest shopping streets in Japan.
