Naha — Shuri Castle

那覇・首里城

Compare on the map

What to look for

Compare the hilltop grounds of Shuri Castle with Naha’s surrounding streets. The selected 1970s imagery predates the 1992 reconstruction; check the official park website for current restoration and access information.

Okinawa was not part of Japan for most of its history. It was the Ryūkyū Kingdom, trading with China and southeast Asia, and Shuri Castle was its palace.

The castle was destroyed in the Battle of Okinawa in 1945. For decades afterwards a university stood on the site — that is what the 1970s photo shows.

It was rebuilt in 1992, and in 2019 a fire destroyed the main hall again.

For the latest restoration progress and visitor access, consult the official Shurijo Castle Park website.
