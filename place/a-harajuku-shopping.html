Street fashion and distinctive small shops around Takeshita Street.

The pin marks the area, not a particular shop.

Before you go

Takeshita Street starts steps from Harajuku Station and is crowded most days. Cat Street is the calmer, more grown-up alternative, and Meiji Jingu is close by when you need a break.
