# 1913 Shinsekai plan — first visual pass

The 1913 `新世界平面図` appears in a [modern article](https://radonna.biz/blog/new_world/) as a low-resolution reproduction from *大阪新名所新世界写真帖*. It was visually inspected in Chrome on 2026-09-30. The reproduced image has no cleared reuse license, so this file records observations only. Do not copy the image into the product or repository. Refer to ledger S016.

The printed plan is **south-up**. From the image top toward bottom (geographic south toward north), it shows a reserved/development block, theatre blocks, Luna Park, a row of halls, the first tower on 通天通, a half-round garden/円街, then the three radial streets. The first tower and the half-round garden are distinct sites. This agrees with the [historian-supervised Shinsekai Fest history](https://www.shinsekaifes.jp/%E3%83%92%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC) placing the first tower about 30 m south of the current second tower.

Reading a south-up plan: left on the page corresponds to geographic east, right to west. Facility and road labels are small and several are unclear in this reproduction; the detailed tracing should use the original 1913 album and the illustrated park program at Osaka Prefectural Library, or a demonstrably public-domain high-resolution scan. Do not derive metre-scale building footprints from this reproduction.

## Confirmed topology from this view and independent text

1. Luna Park is south of the first tower. The park is inset within surrounding theatre/entertainment blocks.
2. A transverse street (`通天通`) separates the park-facing hall row from the north district around the tower.
3. The old semicircular garden/円街 is north of the first tower and is approximately the site of the current second tower.
4. The three fan-shaped shopping streets extend north from the semicircular frontage: 恵美須通, 玉水通, 合邦通. The [Osaka City history](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/naniwa/page/0000632322.html) gives their directions and modern equivalents.
5. A large reserved/development block is drawn south of the theatre strip. It is not an opening-day attraction and must not be filled with the later bath or other facilities without date-specific proof.

The plan is an artist/developer plan rather than a geodetic survey. T3 can use it for relative placement but needs modern street alignment, photo control points, and the original plan to make georeferenced geometry. `layout-1912.geojson` accordingly has no surveyed building/road lines.
