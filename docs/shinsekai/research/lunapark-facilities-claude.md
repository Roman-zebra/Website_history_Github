## Second pass (16:49): 1914 『大阪独案内』 (NDL 952032 frame 95, left page, lower text; login-free IIIF, read from the page image)
- 「ルナパークは敷地四千有余坪にして中央見付けに大〔築山〕を築き山頂に高さ百□尺の白塔中空に聳へ、山麓には**夫婦池**ありて白塔下の一大瀑布は之に流入し、其附近は**日本式庭園**と為せり。園の周囲は余興館にして**埃及館、不思議館、清華殿、動物館**となす。動物館には獅子、虎、象、豹、白熊、狒々等…」
- Facilities around the park's edge: Egypt-kan, Fushigi-kan, Seika-den and a **zoo hall** (lion, tiger, elephant, leopard, polar bear, baboons…).
- A **Japanese-style garden** by the pond.
- The White Tower's height digit after 百 is unclear. The 1912 text gives 150 shaku.

## Pond names: evidence, not a merge
- The 1912 text calls the ground **below the White Tower's waterfall** 「真澄池」 (ringed by fountains, five-colour underwater lamps).
- The 1914 text says the **waterfall under the White Tower flows into 夫婦池** at the foot of the mound.
- Both describe the basin that receives the waterfall, but with different names two years apart. That could be a renaming, two connected basins, or an author's variation.
- Per Codex's rule they **stay distinct** until a plan or caption shows the same outline. This is noted as supporting evidence for a later check against the 1913 developer album plan.
