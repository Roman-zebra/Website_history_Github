# Contractor retrospective: 200-shaku tower statement

Obayashi Corporation's online edition of the [1940 *Obayashi Yoshigoro Den*, Shinsekai construction section](https://www.obayashi.co.jp/chronicle/yoshigoroden/t5c3s5.html) contains an account by Obayashi executive **白杉嘉明三** of the company's work. The [colophon page](https://www.obayashi.co.jp/chronicle/yoshigoroden/t10.html) confirms the original **1940** publication year. The account says a **200-shaku iron tower** was built at the park entrance and describes a raised point facing it at a distance of **200 shaku**, linked by an aerial car. At 1 shaku ≈ 0.303 m, each rounded figure is about **60.6 m**. It also dates construction from September 1911 to June 1912.

This is a participant-side **retrospective narrative published 28 years after opening**, not a 1912 construction drawing, survey, instrument reading or stated ground-to-tip measurement. Its reference levels and whether the two numbers were rounded are unspecified. In particular, the second 200-shaku figure is a **separation** between tower and raised point, not another tower height. The contractor's first 200-shaku figure differs from the [1924 table's 250-shaku height](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/917132/1/25) and the [1922 guide's sea-elevation wording](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/964429/1/170). No one source resolves the discrepancy or sets the as-built 1912 tip elevation. Claude's photo scenarios near 63–68 m remain conditional camera fits and are not validated by this rounded recollection.

Claude's [publication-ordered comparison](tower-study-review-claude.md) §18 now reports a **250-shaku height statement in a 1912 guide**, followed by both 250 and 300 shaku in one 1914 guide. The NDL catalogue date and a full-text hit for `二百五十尺` on the reported 1912 page were independently checked in Chrome; Codex has not completed a line-by-line scan transcription. The 1940 recollection cannot establish an earlier 200-shaku design. The contemporary guides are inconsistent and none states a measured ground-to-tip datum.

Use this account as an additional height and rough ropeway-separation lead for T3; keep the Blender study's `--height` and passage-depth parameters adjustable. Do not position the White Tower or ropeway cabin at a 60.6 m map offset until the original dated plan and photo camera are checked.
