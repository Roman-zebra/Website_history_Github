Planning a Japan trip from Singapore: a city walk with context

For a shorter city break, choose one area near your accommodation and explore it in detail. Fukuoka, Osaka and Yokohama offer three different starting points: a street-and-waterway landscape, castle grounds, and a changing waterfront. Pick one that fits your visit; they are not three stops on a single walk.

Before you explore

Japan is one hour ahead of Singapore. Allow time for the actual journey from your accommodation, rather than relying on straight-line map distances. This guide is in English, and a Simplified Chinese entry is also available. Choose the language you prefer; the site does not use your country as proof of your language.
