Planning a Japan trip from the USA: history and walking maps

For a first Japan itinerary, use the map to connect the places you have heard of with the streets around them. Start with Asakusa in Tokyo, the hillside around Kiyomizu-dera in Kyoto, or Hiroshima’s Peace Memorial Park. Each is a separate starting point; this is not a one-day route between three distant cities.

Before you explore

When planning from the United States, check the arrival date as well as the flight time: the date can change when crossing the Pacific. Japan uses one time zone. Save a few places for each overnight base and check actual transport separately. Historical aerial images explain the layout; they do not show current closures or live conditions.

Open the map, choose an available photograph period and move the comparison slider. Follow the same river, road or temple grounds in both views. You can save places in this browser without an account.
