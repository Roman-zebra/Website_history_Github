Before you explore

Japan stays on the same time throughout the year, while the UK changes between GMT and British Summer Time. Check the time difference for your travel dates when booking. The maps use kilometres; nearby-place distances are straight lines, not walking or rail distances. Confirm actual routes before combining destinations.
